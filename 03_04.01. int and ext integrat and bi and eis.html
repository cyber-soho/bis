2. The Arithmetic of Growth

As a company grows, how we "wire" systems together matters. Point-to-Point (P2P) connections grow exponentially (n²), while Hub-and-Spoke models stay linear (n). Use the calculator below to see why large enterprises shift to middleware like an ESB (Enterprise Service Bus) to avoid "Spaghetti Architecture."

3. The BI Pipeline (ETL)

Raw data is messy. Business Intelligence (BI) relies on ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) to clean transactional data before it hits the Data Warehouse. This allows us to separate OLTP (fast live payments) from OLAP (slow analytical questions).
