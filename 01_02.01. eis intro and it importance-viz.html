The Strategic Importance of IT

Technology may be a commodity, but how you use it is not. IT stops being a cost and becomes strategic when it changes what the business can do that rivals cannot. We evaluate this using Porter's frameworks.

1 Porter's Five Forces

Diagnose industry risk. The chart visualizes a hypothetical industry under threat. Click the buttons below to see how IT shifts the balance.
