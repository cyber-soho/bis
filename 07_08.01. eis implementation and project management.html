Enterprise Information Systems (EIS)

Welcome to the interactive study companion. This dashboard translates the core concepts of EIS Implementation and Project Management into explorable tools. The purpose of this section is to set the context for our fictional case study, Northern Roast Coffee Co., whose data powers the simulations you are about to use.

Meet Northern Roast Coffee Co.

A local coffee roaster with one roastery and three cafés. Currently running on a tangle of spreadsheets and supplier invoices, they are preparing to implement a proper EIS to tie every department together.

  • Users: 40 staff members
  • Locations: 4 (1 roastery, 3 cafés)
  • Current Pain: $3,000/month wasted on duplicate entry

Key Vocabulary

  • EIS (Enterprise Information System): The big, company-wide software that ties departments together on shared data.
  • ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning): The most common flavour of EIS (finance, HR, sales, inventory together). Market examples: SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Salesforce.

Risk Management Register

The purpose of this section is to demonstrate how project managers prioritize threats. You will interact with a live Risk Register. Adjust the 'Likelihood' and 'Impact' dropdowns for each risk scenario. The application will instantly multiply these values to generate a severity score, re-coloring and re-sorting the list to ensure the most critical threats float to the top for immediate action.

ID Risk Description Likelihood (1-5) Impact (1-5) Score Severity

Critical Success Factors (CSFs) are risks turned inside out.

For example, if "Old records won't map cleanly" is a Critical Risk, your CSF is "Solid data-quality and migration plan." Manage the top of your sorted list above to secure project success.
