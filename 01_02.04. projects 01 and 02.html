Enterprise Information Systems: Foundation Project

Welcome to your interactive project brief. The goal of this task is to look at software through a business lens. By the end, you will be able to evaluate any company's systems, map their architecture, and build a compelling financial case for technology investment.

⏱ Estimated Effort: ~4 Hours
📁 Output: 3 Markdown Files + 1 Chart + 1 Script

Project Deliverables

Here is exactly what you need to build and submit. This section breaks down the three parts of the assignment, detailing the required outputs for each. Ensure you have chosen one single real-world organisation to analyze across all three parts.

Part A

20% Weight

Map the Enterprise

Describe your chosen organisation's systems using the building-blocks and pyramid models. Identify a data silo and propose an integration opportunity.

Required Outputs:
  • Org Profile & Source
  • Building Blocks Table
  • Pyramid Layer Table
  • Silo/Integration Pitch
  • Visual Systems Map (Diagram)

Part B

20% Weight

The Strategic Case

Argue whether IT is a strategic advantage for your organisation using Porter's frameworks and the Carr "IT Doesn't Matter" debate.

Required Outputs:
  • Competitor framing
  • Five Forces Analysis Table
  • Value Chain impact mapping
  • Response to Carr's argument

Part C

60% Weight

EIS Business Case

Propose a concrete EIS initiative to solve the silo from Part A. Justify it strategically and prove it financially using the ROI model.

Required Outputs:
  • Initiative Problem & Scope
  • Two candidate products (1 Open)
  • Financial Model (Payback/ROI)
  • Risks & Ethics (PIPEDA)
  • Executive Elevator Pitch
