CASE STUDY: NORTHERN ROAST

From Spreadsheets to Enterprise Systems

Maya's notebook at Northern Roast Coffee Co. has exploded into forty quarrelling spreadsheets. To scale from four cafés to a wholesale empire, she needs an Enterprise Information System (EIS). This dashboard tracks the rigorous acquisition journey — five links in a chain that ensure Maya buys a solution that builds her future, rather than bankrupting it.

Internal Integration

Connecting departments so inventory, finance, and sales speak one truth.

External Integration

Using APIs to sync with green-bean suppliers and POS terminals.

1. The Strategic Fork

The first major decision is the choice between building custom software, buying off-the-shelf, or renting a SaaS solution. This section allows you to explore the Weighted Decision Matrix. By scoring criteria like 'Fit' and 'Total Cost of Ownership', we can visualize which acquisition path aligns best with Northern Roast's constraints.

2. The Human Engine (RACI)

An EIS project dies when responsibility is fuzzy. The RACI Matrix clarifies who is Responsible (R), Accountable (A), Consulted (C), and Informed (I). Use this interactive grid to assign roles. Remember the Golden Rule: Exactly one Accountable person per task.
